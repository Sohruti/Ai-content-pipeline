# Content Strategy

**Topic:** 87% of AI Deployments Are Delayed by Governance, Not Technology

## Business Angle

Addressing the significant delay in AI deployments due to governance issues, rather than technological limitations, to unlock business value and productivity

## Target Audience

CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, Heads of AI, Innovation Leaders, and Digital Transformation Leaders

## Messaging

87% of AI deployments are delayed by governance, not technology. Effective data governance is crucial for successful AI adoption and unlocking business value. Prioritize privacy and security to overcome compliance and operational challenges.

## Content Goal

Thought leadership and awareness, highlighting the importance of governance in AI adoption and positioning 2OS as a trusted solution

## CTA

Learn more about how 2OS's Private AI Infrastructure can help your organization overcome governance challenges and unlock the full potential of AI

## Tone

Authoritative and educational, with a focus on providing valuable insights and solutions to a critical business problem
