# Content Strategy

**Topic:** 87% of AI Deployments Are Delayed by Governance, Not Technology

## Business Angle

Highlighting the importance of governance in AI deployment and how it can impact an organization's ability to successfully integrate AI solutions, positioning 2OS as a trusted partner in addressing these challenges

## Target Audience

CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, Heads of AI, Heads of Innovation, and Digital Transformation Leaders

## Messaging

Emphasize the operational trust gap between AI ambitions and confidence in governance, and how 2OS's Private AI Infrastructure can help bridge this gap by providing a secure and compliant AI infrastructure

## Content Goal

Thought Leadership and Trust building

## CTA

Encourage leaders to prioritize governance in their AI strategies and invite them to learn more about 2OS's approach to privacy-first AI infrastructure

## Tone

Educational and consultative, with a focus on highlighting the risks of neglecting governance in AI deployment
