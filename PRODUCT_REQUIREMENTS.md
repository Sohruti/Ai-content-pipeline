# Product Requirements

## Product Name
2OS GTM Content Operating System

## Problem Statement
Enterprise AI startups struggle to consistently create thought leadership content that resonates with CXO-level buyers. Generic content doesn't build trust. Technical content doesn't drive business conversations.

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Founder/CEO of 2OS (Udit Agrawal)
- **Secondary**: GTM team members

## Target Audience (Readers)
- CEOs
- CIOs
- CTOs
- Heads of AI
- Innovation Leaders
- Digital Transformation Leaders

## Core Requirements

### 1. Knowledge Management
- Store and index company knowledge (website, blogs, founder posts, customer stories)
- Enable semantic search over knowledge base
- Automatically update when new content is added

### 2. Research Automation
- Research topics using web search
- Analyze industry trends
- Track competitor positioning
- Surface relevant news

### 3. Strategy Development
- Determine business angle for content
- Identify target persona
- Define messaging and goals
- Set content tone and type

### 4. Content Creation
- Match founder's writing style
- Create compelling story structures
- Generate LinkedIn-optimized posts
- Maintain authenticity throughout

### 5. Quality Assurance
- Score content against quality criteria
- Ensure business-first messaging
- Verify founder voice authenticity
- Auto-approve or request revisions

## Success Metrics
- Content generated in < 5 minutes
- Approval score >= 9/10 on first iteration
- Consistent founder voice across all content
- Business-first messaging (not technology-first)

## Out of Scope
- Social media scheduling/publishing
- Analytics and engagement tracking
- Multi-platform content (LinkedIn only for MVP)
- Image/media generation
