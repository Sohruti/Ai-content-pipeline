## Content Strategy

### Posting Frequency
- 3-5 posts per week
- Consistency over quantity
- Quality always wins

### Content Mix
- 40% Thought Leadership (original insights)
- 30% Industry Analysis (trends, news)
- 20% Founder Perspective (opinions, lessons)
- 10% Customer Stories (proof, outcomes)

### Best Times to Post
- Tuesday-Thursday
- 8-10 AM or 5-7 PM (target audience timezone)
- Test and optimize based on engagement

## Content Framework

### Hook Patterns
1. Bold Statement: "Most AI projects fail. Here's why."
2. Question: "What if privacy was the feature, not the bug?"
3. Contrarian: "Move fast and break things doesn't work in Enterprise AI."
4. Story: "I just realized something about Enterprise AI."

### Post Structure
1. **Hook** (1-2 lines) → Stop the scroll
2. **Context** (1-2 lines) → Set up the problem
3. **Insight** (3-5 lines) → Share your perspective
4. **Lesson** (2-3 lines) → Business takeaway
5. **CTA** (1 line) → Drive engagement

### Writing Rules
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences)
- Line breaks between sections
- No jargon overload
- No corporate speak
- No hashtag spam (0-3 max)
- No "I'm excited to announce..."
- Always lead with business value

## Engagement Strategy

### Comments
- Respond to every comment within 24 hours
- Ask follow-up questions
- Add value, don't just acknowledge

### Connections
- Connect with target buyers
- Personalize connection requests
- Engage with their content first

### Groups
- Join relevant Enterprise AI groups
- Share insights, not promotions
- Build relationships organically
