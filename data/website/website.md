# Second Order Systems (2OS) - Company Overview

## About 2OS

Second Order Systems (2OS) is an Enterprise AI startup focused on building AI infrastructure that enables enterprises to deploy, manage, and scale AI solutions with privacy and security built-in from the ground up.

## Mission

To make Enterprise AI accessible, secure, and actionable for organizations that cannot afford to compromise on data privacy or governance.

## Core Belief

AI should work for the enterprise without requiring enterprises to sacrifice their data sovereignty. Privacy is not a feature - it's an architectural decision.

## Key Differentiators

- **Privacy-First Architecture**: Built from the ground up with privacy by design
- **Enterprise-Grade Security**: Designed for regulated industries
- **AI Infrastructure**: Not just another AI tool, but the infrastructure layer
- **Second-Order Thinking**: Going beyond immediate solutions to understand second-order effects of AI decisions
